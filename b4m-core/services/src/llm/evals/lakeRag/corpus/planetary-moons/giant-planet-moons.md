# Moon Counts of Jupiter and Saturn

Moon counts for the giant planets change as survey telescopes find small, distant moons. A moon is counted here once its orbit has been confirmed and it has been recognised by the International Astronomical Union.

Saturn has 146 confirmed moons, the most of any planet in the solar system. Most of the recent additions are small irregular moons only a few kilometres across.

Jupiter has 95 confirmed moons. The four Galilean moons account for almost all of the mass orbiting Jupiter; the rest are small bodies, many of them likely captured asteroids.
