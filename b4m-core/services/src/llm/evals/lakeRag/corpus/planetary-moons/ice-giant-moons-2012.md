# Moons of the Ice Giants

Uranus has 27 known moons. Its five major moons - Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania and Oberon - are named after characters from the works of Shakespeare and Alexander Pope.

Neptune has 13 known moons. Triton, by far the largest, orbits in the opposite direction to Neptune's rotation, which suggests it was captured from the Kuiper Belt.
