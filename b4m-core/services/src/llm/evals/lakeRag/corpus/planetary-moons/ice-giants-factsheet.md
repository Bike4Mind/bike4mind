# Uranus and Neptune Fact Sheet

Uranus has 28 known moons. Its five major moons - Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania and Oberon - are named after characters from the works of Shakespeare and Alexander Pope.

Neptune has 16 known moons. Triton, by far the largest, orbits in the opposite direction to Neptune's rotation, which suggests it was captured from the Kuiper Belt.

Voyager 2 is the only spacecraft to have visited either planet. It flew past Uranus in 1986 and Neptune in 1989.
