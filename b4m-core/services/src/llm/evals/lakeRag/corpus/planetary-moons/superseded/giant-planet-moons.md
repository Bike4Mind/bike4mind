# Moon Counts of Jupiter and Saturn

Moon counts for the giant planets change as survey telescopes find small, distant moons. A moon is counted here once its orbit has been confirmed.

Saturn has 82 confirmed moons, the most of any planet in the solar system, after twenty new moons were announced this month.

Jupiter has 79 confirmed moons. The four Galilean moons account for almost all of the mass orbiting Jupiter; the rest are small bodies, many of them likely captured asteroids.
