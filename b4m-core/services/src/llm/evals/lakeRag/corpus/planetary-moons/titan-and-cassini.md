# Titan and the Cassini Mission

Titan is Saturn's largest moon. The Dutch astronomer Christiaan Huygens discovered it in 1655. Titan is the only moon in the solar system with a thick atmosphere, which is made mostly of nitrogen, and it has lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane on its surface.

The Cassini-Huygens mission was a joint project of NASA, the European Space Agency and the Italian Space Agency. The spacecraft launched on October 15, 1997, and entered orbit around Saturn in 2004.

The Huygens probe separated from the orbiter and landed on Titan on January 14, 2005. It was the first landing on a moon in the outer solar system.

The Cassini orbiter studied Saturn, its rings and its moons for thirteen years. The mission ended on September 15, 2017, when the spacecraft was deliberately steered into Saturn's atmosphere so that it could not contaminate a moon that might harbour life.
