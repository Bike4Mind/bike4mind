# Definition of the Kilogram

The kilogram is defined by fixing the numerical value of the Planck constant. The definition took effect on May 20, 2019, World Metrology Day, after the General Conference on Weights and Measures voted for it in November 2018.

Because the definition rests on a constant of nature, any suitably equipped laboratory can realise the kilogram, for example with a Kibble balance, which compares mechanical power with electrical power.
