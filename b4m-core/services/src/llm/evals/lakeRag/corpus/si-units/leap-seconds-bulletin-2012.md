# Leap Second Bulletin

A leap second was inserted at the end of June 30, 2012. Atomic time (TAI) is now ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) by 35 seconds.

That makes 25 leap seconds inserted since the current system began in 1972. Leap seconds keep UTC within 0.9 seconds of UT1, the time scale based on the rotation of the Earth.
