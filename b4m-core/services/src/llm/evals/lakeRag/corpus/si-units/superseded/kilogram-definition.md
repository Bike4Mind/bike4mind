# Definition of the Kilogram

The kilogram is defined as the mass of the International Prototype of the Kilogram, a platinum-iridium cylinder kept at the BIPM near Paris. The definition has been in effect since 1889.

National copies of the prototype are periodically compared with it. Those comparisons show the copies drifting by tens of micrograms, which is one reason metrologists are working towards a definition based on a constant of nature.
