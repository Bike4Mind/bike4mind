# UTC and Leap Seconds

Atomic time (TAI) is ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) by 37 seconds. The most recent leap second was inserted at the end of December 31, 2016.

In total, 27 leap seconds have been inserted since the current system began in 1972.

In November 2022 the General Conference on Weights and Measures decided to stop inserting leap seconds by 2035, allowing the difference between UTC and Earth-rotation time to grow beyond one second.
