# Congressional Apportionment Results

The apportionment counts released today set how many House seats each state holds.

Texas gained two seats and now has 38 seats in the House of Representatives, the largest gain of any state.

California lost a seat for the first time in its history and now has 52 seats, though it remains the state with the largest delegation.
