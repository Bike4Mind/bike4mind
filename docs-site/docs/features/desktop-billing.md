# Desktop Billing

Sign in, open your account menu, and select Billing. The screen shows your personal credit balance, current subscription, available Professional plans, and one-time credit packs for the server you signed in to.

## Subscribe

1. Choose an available Professional plan and select Subscribe.
2. Complete checkout in your system browser.
3. Return to Desktop and select Refresh to read your confirmed plan and balance from the server.

Opening checkout does not confirm a payment. If you close checkout without paying, select I canceled or closed checkout to choose a plan again. An existing subscription blocks a second checkout until it is canceled or its incomplete payment expires. Use Billing in the web app to change or cancel an existing subscription.

## Buy credits

1. Select a one-time pack under Top up credits.
2. Select Buy to open the secure payment window inside Desktop.
3. Complete payment, then close the window to refresh your balance.

Credit packs do not require a subscription. Payment methods that require visiting another payment-provider site are not offered inside the desktop payment window. Completed payments can take a moment to appear in your balance; refresh before trying again if the outcome is unclear.

## Accounts and availability

Billing belongs to your signed-in account on the selected server. Switching account or server clears the previous billing view and closes an open credit-payment window.

If billing is disabled or the plans and subscription status cannot be read, checkout is unavailable. Use Refresh to retry. An unavailable balance is shown as Unavailable rather than as zero.
